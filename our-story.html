Our Heritage & Craft

Sown by hand, baked by wood fire.

Our journey began with a simple jar of wild yeast starter, cultured from the air of our local valleys, and a deep dissatisfaction with fast-rising modern commercial bread. We wanted to return to a time when baking was an act of patience, connection, and true nourishment.

At Crust & Crumb Sourdough, we have dedicated ourselves to the preservation of heirloom flour varieties and the slow art of wild fermentation. Every single loaf we sell passes through a thirty-six-hour cold fermentation process, allowing the grain's natural flavors to mature and become easier to digest.

We don’t use additives, commercial yeast, or artificial dough conditioners. Just freshly milled stoneground grains, mineral-rich spring water, sea salt, and our living wild cultures.

The 36-Hour Rhythm

How we bring grain to life

We respect the natural timeline of sourdough. By giving our dough the space and hours it demands, we achieve complex aromas and superior textures that fast baking methods simply cannot replicate.

01

Stone Ground Sourcing

We source heirloom and ancient wheat varieties from regional organic farmers who prioritize soil health. By milling these grains on cool granite stones, we preserve the nutrient-dense germ and fragrant botanical oils that give our bread its distinct identity.

02

Slow Autolyse & Ferment

Flour and water rest together to naturally initiate gluten development. We then introduce our mature mother starter, hand-folding the dough over many hours. A 36-hour cold ferment allows lactic acid bacteria to unlock the grain’s complex sugars.

03

Wood-Fired Radiance

Our heavy deck oven is loaded with firewood hours before dawn. Loaves are hand-scored and baked directly on the hot brick hearths. Intense radiant heat gelatinizes the starches, resulting in a blistered, deeply caramelized, and beautifully bitter crust.
