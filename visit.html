Follow the smell of fresh bread to our door.

Every morning before dawn, we fire up our stone hearth deck oven. By seven, the racks are loaded with dark, caramelized crusts and flaky croissants. We bake in small batches, and when the shelves are empty, we close for the day.

Fresh bakes, pulled from the bricks daily

Reserve your daily bakes.

Our sourdough loaves take thirty-six hours of slow fermentation to develop their distinctive deep flavor. Because of this slow, hands-on craft, quantities are strictly limited. You can pre-order your favorite sandwich loaves, baguettes, or sweet pastries using our simple pickup inquiry below.

Pre-Order & General Inquiry

Send us a note to reserve a loaf or ask about seasonal pastry catering. We respond same-day before 4 PM.

1

Send your request

Place your reservation inquiry before 2:00 PM the prior day. This allows our bakers to adjust our overnight dough schedules to fit your loaf.

2

Receive confirmation

We will confirm your order details and specify your pickup window. No payment is required until you hold the warm loaf at the counter.

3

Early-morning pickup

Arrive at 142 Hearthside Lane, Old Town District during your window. Walk straight to the registers, give your name, and enjoy your morning bakes.
