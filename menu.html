Honest Craftsmanship

How Our Flour Becomes Bread

We do not use commercial yeast, additives, or dough conditioners. Our process relies entirely on pure well water, stone-milled grains, mineral sea salt, and time.

1

Sourcing the Heritage Grain

Our grains are cultivated using organic methods. We select specific heritage wheat and rye varieties that retain natural nutrients and deep agricultural character.

2

Cold 36-Hour Fermentation

Slow fermentation breaks down starches and gluten naturally. This thorough breakdown makes our sourdough far gentler on your digestive system.

3

Wood-Fired Steam Baking

Baked directly on the volcanic stone decks of our custom hearth. Trapped humidity creates high-intensity steam, giving each loaf its signature crisp, thick crust.
